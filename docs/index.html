From public crash reports to robotaxi validation targets

Zoox is expanding driverless service and needs to spend limited test time on the right scenarios. This case study cleans NHTSA's crash reports, finds where Zoox's crash mix differs from Waymo's, tests whether those differences are real, and turns them into simulation test cases.

    Chapter 3 · Statistical modeling

    Which differences are real, and how sure can we be?

    Left: shares with 95% Wilson intervals and Fisher's exact tests, Holm-corrected across all seven comparisons. Right: a Bayesian beta-binomial model (flat prior) that gives the probability the robotaxi's share really exceeds Waymo's.

    Curves are posterior densities scaled to equal height. Waymo's is narrow because it rests on 2,309 reports; the robotaxi's is wide at 48.
